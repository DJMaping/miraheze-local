# 💰 Economy

Money, trade and development across Andah. The reserve currency is the **lahn**.

**Dashboard:** [[2 Dashboards/Countries.base|Countries]] – sort by `gdp_ppp`, `gdp_nominal`
or `hdi` to rank nations; [[2 Dashboards/Continents.base|Continents]] aggregates by region.

## Canon data sources

Reuse these in-universe bodies rather than inventing competing ones:

- **Global Trade Union (GTU)** – GDP, trade, ports
- **World Data Union** / `Census <Country>` – population and area
- **Krali Development Report** – HDI / development index
