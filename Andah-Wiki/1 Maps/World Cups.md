# 🏆 FLLA World Cups

The FLLA World Cup, the world championship of football, contested every four years.

**Dashboard:** [[2 Dashboards/World Cups.base|World Cup editions]] – every edition with host,
champion, runner-up and top scorer; sort by year.

## Reading the editions

Each edition note carries `year`, `host`, `champion`, `runner_up`, `third_place`,
`top_scorer` and `best_player` in its properties, so the dashboard is the fastest way
to scan winners and hosts across the whole series.
