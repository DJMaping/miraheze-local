
The '''mayor of Vurahi''' is the head of the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government local government] of [[Vurahi]], [[Areoix Lie]]; currently that government takes the form of the  [[The city of Vurahi|the City of Vurahi]] .

The mayoral position was established on 7 12th 1717.

In the past, the position of [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor mayor] has varied between that of an executive mayor actively governing the city and that of a figurehead mayor with a mostly ceremonial role. The current mayor is [[Vera Namqic]] of the [[DAEL]]. The [[Deputy Mayor of Vurahi]] is elected by the [[Vurahi]] City Council and serves alongside the [[Mayor of Vurahi]].
