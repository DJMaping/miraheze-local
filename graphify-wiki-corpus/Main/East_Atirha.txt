'''East Atirha''' is a sub-continent located in the northern hemisphere. It is bordered the [[Uscea Sea]] and [[Rianian Sea]] to the south. [[Central Atirha]] to the west, [[Srandan Ocean]] to the north, and the [[Rianian Ocean]] to the east. The continent covers around {{convert|3,956,846|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land, making it the 15th largest sub-continent in the world, with a total of about 107,817,357 as of 1765, making it also the 20th largest sub-continent by population.
