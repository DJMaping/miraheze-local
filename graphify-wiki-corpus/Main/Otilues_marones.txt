'''Otilues Marones''' (born 18 3rd 1732) is a [[arten]] [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politician politician] serving as the [[Deputy Mayor of Vurahi]] since 12th 1758. A former rugby union footballer, his usual position was prop, and he played for the Springboks. He played for the [[Vurahi Warriors]] in the Super 1744 between 1748.
