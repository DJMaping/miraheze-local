'''Central Atirha''' is a sub-continent located in the northern hemisphere. It is bordered the [[Uscea Sea]] to the south, [[West Atirha]] to the west, the [[Srandan Ocean]] to the north, and the [[East Atirha]] to the east. The continent covers around {{convert|6,652,554|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land, making it the 5th largest sub-continent in the world, with a total of about 326,379,040 as of 1765, making it also the 6th largest sub-continent by population.
