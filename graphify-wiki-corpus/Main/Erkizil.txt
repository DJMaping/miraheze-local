'''Erkizil''', officially known as the '''Republic of Erkizil''', is a nation in [[West Quia]]. It covers an area of [[List of countries and dependencies by area|522,616 km2 (201,783 sq mi),]] making it the twelfth-largest country in Quia. The country is bordered by [[Ztesh]] to the east, [[Quidic]] to the south, the [[Ashanian Sea]] to the west, and the [[Hinsakian Ocean]] to the north. The capital and largest city is [[Tiraqsi]]; other major urban centres include [[Erosan]], [[Vellikar]], [[Cairiso]], [[Iktavi]], [[Washashi]], and [[Koyah]]. The western regions of the country experience a [[wikipedia:Semi-arid_climate|semi-arid climate]] in the highlands, while the central and eastern areas have [[wikipedia:Tropical_and_subtropical_grasslands,_savannas,_and_shrublands|tropical savanna]] and [[wikipedia:Tropical_monsoon_climate|tropical monsoon]] climates, characterised by large amounts of rainfall along the coast.

The area that is now modern-day Erkizil was inhabited by [[several tribal states]] and kingdoms, which frequently changed, merged, or collapsed. For the [[Aksalshai tribes]], the region was known as [[Acsalshai language|Acsalshai]], meaning "dry lands" It was the most sparsely populated area in the [[Ankuano|Ankuano subcontinent]] due to its more arid and hilly gerography. 

Early civilisations in the area included the [[Ankusano-Elaian langauge family|Ankusano-Elaian]] speaking [[Krakkaashai]] city-states in western [[Ankuano]]. In the 9th century BFA, the [[Aksalshai Kingdom]] was founded around the upper Lawady valley, many smaller kingdoms reigned through the 3rd century BFA till the 1st century AFA. Following the establishment of the [[Alleuran Empire]] in 133, the [[Aksalshai language]] and culture and ____ slowly became dominant in the country. The Kingdom fell to [[First Ast's invasions|Ast's invasions]], and several warring states emerged. In the 8th century, reunified by the [[Vermmiaq dynasty]], the dynasty became the [[Vermmiaq Empire]] for a short period. In the mid 12th century [[Zaranta Kingdom]] was founded in 1160 AFA, starting the golden age of trade in the area. After the collapse of the Ast-Kauria, the [[Seecan Empire]] ruled over an area that included modern Erkizil and briefly controlled parts if [[Ztesh]] and [[Quidic]], before coming under an [[Ashain|Ashaini colony]] of [[Meblicji]] in 1630 AFA in the Division of Ankuano. The Ashani created the [[Xial Co]], which acted as an administrative system for the Quian colonies. During the [[Red war]] the modern area of Erkizil was [[heavily destroyed]] by the fighting between [[Emara]] and [[Ashain]], causing the [[Erkizil famine in 1693]], where an estimated 2.5-5 million died from [[wikipedia:Malnutrition|malnutrition]].

After the [[Ashani empire]] was dismantled in the [[Treaty of Freedom]] in 1695, [[Ankuano]] was partitioned into four independent nations: [[Ztesh]], Erkizil, [[Quidic]] and [[Wundry]]. A large-scale loss of life and an [[unprecedented migration]] accompanied the [[partition]].

Erkizil has been a [[wikipedia:Unitary_state|unitary republic]] since 1765, governed through a democratic [[wikipedia:Parliamentary_system|parliamentary system]]. It is a pluralistic, [[wikipedia:Multilingualism|multilingual]] and [[wikipedia:Multiracial_people|multi-ethnic]] society. Erkizil's population grew from 24 million in 1700 to over 90 million in 1765. During this time, its [[List of countries by GDP (nominal) per capita|nominal per capita income]] increased from {{lahn}}____ annually to {{lahn}}1,817, and its literacy rate from 16.6% to 74%. A comparatively destitute country in 1951. Among the socio-economic challenges Erkizil faces are gender inequality [[wikipedia:Undernutrition_in_children|child malnutrition]], and rising levels of [[wikipedia:Air_pollution|air pollution]]. Erkizil's land is [[wikipedia:Megadiverse_countries|megadiverse]] with four biodiversity hotspots. Erkizil's wildlife, which has traditionally been viewed with tolerance in its [[Erkizilian Culture|culture]], is supported in [[Erkizilian protected habitats|protected habitats]].
