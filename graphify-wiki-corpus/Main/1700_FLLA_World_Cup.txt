The '''1700 [[FLLA]] World Cup''' was the 8th edition of the [[FLLA World Cup]], the quadrennial international [[Arraby]] tournament contested by the senior men's national teams of the member associations of the [[FLLA]]. It was held in [[Raledria]] across [[Atirha]], and was won by [[Raledria]], who defeated [[United Delet]] 3-1 in the final.

The 1700 edition is widely regarded as a watershed in the competition's history. Held in [[Atirha]] for the first time, it was the first tournament contested under the new title '''FLLA World Cup''', a renaming formally adopted by the [[FLLA]] Council on 14 February 1699 to reflect the participation of teams from all then-existing continental confederations. The tournament resumed the competition after the longest hiatus in its history, three consecutive editions ([[1688 FLLA World Cup|1688]], [[1692 FLLA World Cup|1692]] and [[1696 FLLA World Cup|1696]]) having been cancelled because of the [[Red War]]. The final at the Tirna National Stadium drew an estimated crowd of 38,000, the largest single-match attendance recorded at any international fixture to that date.
