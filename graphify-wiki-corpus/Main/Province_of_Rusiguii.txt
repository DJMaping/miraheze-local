The '''Province of Rusiguii''' was a Merelan colony and later an Etirhan province in [[Etirha]]. It was named after the Emaran town of [[Ruzigy]] in southern Emara by [[Phiastysu Mason (governor)|Captain Phiastysu Mason]] in 1520, its first named proprietor. In 1642, the province established an independent state and government, the [[Rusiguii Republic]], and joined with [[Provinces of Etirha]] to form the [[Etirha|United Provinces of Etirha]].

Ayumans first settled Rusiguii in the 1330s, and the province consisted for many years of a small number of communities along the seacoast. In 1353, the area was sold to the [[Praesyu|Kingdom of Praesyu]], creating the [[Colony of Saeffak]] for 12,000 Kund, until Lycroa took the colony in 1395 during the [[6 Year War]] and issued a colonial charter combining the land with its [[Colony of Pelan]]. After the [[Lycroan Madness]] in the 1520s, the territory was taken by [[Emara]] into the [[Rusiguii Colony]] in 1525. Following the [[Seniaaii wars]], Emaran's control over the territory vanished, and on October 7, 1544, Rusiguii was temporarily integrated into [[Raledria]], then granted to Merela Sta and organized as the [[Province Rusiguii]].

The province's economy was dominated by [[Wikipedia:Timber|timber]], [[Wikipedia:fishing|fishing]] and [[Ala]]. The timber trade, although lucrative, was a subject of conflict with the Crown, which sought to reserve the best trees for use as ship [[Wikipedia:Mast (sailing)|masts]]. Although [[Lycroa|Lycroans]] from [[Pelan]] ruled the province for many years, the Rusiguii population was religiously diverse, in part originating in its early years from refugees fleeing opposition to religious differences in [[Atirha]].
