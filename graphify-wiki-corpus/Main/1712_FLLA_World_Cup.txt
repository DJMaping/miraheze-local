The '''1712 [[FLLA]] World Cup''' was the 11th edition of the [[FLLA World Cup]], the quadrennial international [[Arraby]] tournament contested by the senior men's national teams of the member associations of the [[FLLA]]. It was held in [[Praesyu]] across [[Ayuma]], and was won by [[Raledria]], who defeated [[Easuhura]] 4-2 in the final.

Hosted by [[Praesyu]], the 1712 edition was won by [[Raledria]] who defeated [[Easuhura]] 4-2 in the final to claim their second FLLA World Cup title. The tournament featured an open, attacking style of play and produced a per-match goal average not surpassed at any subsequent edition until the [[1748 FLLA World Cup|1748 tournament]]. The Praesyan organising committee staged the final at the country's largest venue in the federal capital.
