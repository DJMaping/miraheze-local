The '''Samarran Mountains''' or Samarrans are a range of mountains forming an line across western [[Alzuria (region)]]. The highest peaks in the Samarran are in the [[Klaia Mountains]], exceeding ________ m closely followed by those in the [[Ventral Samarran]] in [[Acrana]], exceeding _______ m.

The range stretches from the south in [[Zenashan]], then travels north though [[Acrana]] and [[Ealdorii]], and ends in the northern Samarran in [[Praesyu]]. The term [[Outer Samarran]] is frequently used to describe the transition point between the Samarran and [[Amnant mountain]] ranges.

The Samarran provide habitat for the largest Ayuman populations of [[Brown cial|brown Cial]], with the highest concentration in [[Zenashan]], as well as over one-eighth of all [[Ayuman plant species]]. The mountains and their foothills also have many thermal and mineral waters.

== Name ==
In modern times, the range is called Salmarran in [[Acranan Language|Acranan]] and its dieledts, in [[Ealdorii Language|Ealdorii]] and [[Zenashan Language|Zenashan]] called, Loaxan, and in [[Fravalan Language|Fravalan]] its Roslan. Although the toponym was recorded by in the second century AFA, the modern form of the name is a neologism in most languages.

=== Historical names ===
In late [[Vestorian Empire|Vestorian]] documents, the northen Samarran Mountains were referred to as Sarmatici (meaning Samatian Mountains). The Southen Samarran were called Karan, a name that is first recorded in [[Ecossian]] in the third century BFA.

Havacoa ("Sunset Mountains") was its medieval [[Ukhdari Language|Ukhdari]] name. [[Arten Language|Arten]]' chronicles referred to it as "Acranan Mountains". Later sources, such as Dvalie Cetmad and the [[Lycroa|Lycroan]] chronicler Groa Loqa, referred to the wider range as "Ayuman Mountains".

=== Etymology ===
The etymology of the Samarran is not clearly established, but the name "maram" is highly associated with the old [[Vasconian]] tribes called "Vascons" or "Vascs" who lived in an area to the east of the central and southern Samarrans.

== Geography ==
The western slopes catch moisture-laden winds from the [[Nasuanian Sea]] and are thus more fertile and more heavily populated than the eastern slopes. The [[Oarate River]] flows south alongside the range on its eastern verge in the [[Samaru Plain]].

South of Cindrara there is a large northeast-southwest strike-slip fault which separates [[Syyaih Mountain]] from the coastal [[Mount Sialaq|Mount Sialaq,]] in a feature known as the [[Olam Gap]].

For most of recorded history, the mountains formed the border between the peoples of Alzuria and the Acranans and the Zenashan peoples within the [[Zenashan marshes|southern marshes]] of Alzuria.
