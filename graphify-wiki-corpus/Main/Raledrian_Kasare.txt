The '''Raledrian Kasare''' (symbol: {{Raledrian Kasare}}; [[wikipedia:ISO 4217|ISO code]] '''RKA''') is the official currency of [[Raledria]] and one of the two principal '''globally backed reserve currencies''' of [[Andah]], paired with the [[Lahn]] of [[Dahe]]. It is issued by the [[Reserve Bank of Raledria]] and is held in central-bank reserves by the substantial majority of [[Global Trade Union]] member states.

The Raledrian Kasare is the unit of account for Raledrian federal accounts and is one of the two currencies in which the [[Global Trade Union]] publishes parallel comparative tables of international trade and foreign-exchange holdings. It has been a continuously convertible currency since the [[Treaty of Tirna]] of 1612 and is regarded as the senior of the two world reserve currencies by length of continuous convertibility.

==History==

==Reserve status==

The Raledrian Kasare is one of two currencies recognised by the [[Global Trade Union]] as a primary global reserve, the other being the [[Lahn]] of [[Dahe]]. Together with the lahn it accounts for the substantial majority of central-bank foreign-exchange holdings reported to the [[Global Trade Union]] by member states.
