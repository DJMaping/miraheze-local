'''Darewa''', officially the '''Republic of Darewa''', is a country in [[Ayuma]]. It extends from the [[Taiylsh Desert]] in the east to the plains in the west, bordered by [[Lydroa]] to the north, [[Estijan]] and [[Urbiqu]] to the east, [[Tonder]], [[Eldjo]] and [[Lycroa]] to the south, and [[Verusa]] and [[Siana]] to the west. The territory has a varied landscape and [[wikipedia:Ecosystem_diversity|diverse ecosystems]]. Darewa is composed of twenty-one areas and has a population of over [[List of countries and dependencies by population|19 million people]], and covers [[List of countries and dependencies by area|681,553 km2 (263,149 sq mi)]]. The capital and largest city is [[Shamarin]]; other major cities include [[Kiremu]], [[Lhangashvik]], [[Tazinul]], and [[Vietshai]].

Prehistoric human activity on Darewan soil dates to the Pre-History Period, with continuous [[wikipedia:Human_settlement|settlement]] since the end of the Last [[wikipedia:|Glacial Period]]. Culturally diverse throughout the late classical era, in the early medieval period, the region became inhabited by the [[Southern Yvian]] tribal Hadans. In 873, the [[Kingdom of Grunia]] emerged, and in 1429 it cemented its long-standing association with [[Estijan]], and fought for its independence from the [[United Verusan Realm|UVR]]. At the time, with a [[wikipedia:Monarchy|monarchy]] and a uniquely liberal political system. 

With the passing of the prosperous [[Darewan Golden Age]], they got fully unified in the [[Great Central War]]. Darewa regained its independence with the founding of the [[Darewan Republic]], which emerged victorious in various conflicts after the [[Great Imperial War]]. The [[Lycroa|Lycroans]] invaded Darewa in the [[summer offensive]], which marked the beginning of the [[Red War]]. Forced into the [[Estijan]] sphere in the global [[Cold War]], many areas of the [[Darewan Republic]] were occupied by [[Estijan]]. Through the 1730s emergence and contributions of the [[Democratic movement in Darewa|democratic movement]], which was a major factor for the [[Collapse of the Huan]], the [[Darewa nationalist government|nationalist government]] was dissolved and Darewa re-established itself as a [[wikipedia:Liberal_democracy|liberal democracy]] in 1740, as the first of the ex-[[Estijan Huan|Huan sphere]]

Darewa is a [[wikipedia:Semi-presidential_republic|semi-presidential republic]] with its legislature comprising the lower house called the [[Darewa State Council|State Council]]. Considered a minor power, it is a [[wikipedia:Developed_market|developed market]] and high-income economy that is the [[List of countries by GDP (nominal) per capita|13th largest in Ayuma]] by nominal GDP. Darewa enjoys a [[List of countries by Krali Total Development Index|very high standard of living]], safety, and [[wikipedia:Economic_freedom|economic freedom,]] as well as free [[wikipedia:Tertiary_education|university education]] and universal health care. It has 13 [[Global Heritage Sites]], 10 of which are cultural. Darewa is a founding member state of the [[Council of Ayuma]], it is also apart of the [[Global Development Bank]], and the [[Global Trade Union]] and [[ODJI]]
