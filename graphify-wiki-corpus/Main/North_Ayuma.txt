'''North Ayuma''' is a sub-continent located in the northern hemisphere. It is bordered the [[Central Ayuma]] and [[North Acrola]] to the south. [[West Ayuma]] to the west, the [[Srandan Ocean]] to the north, and the [[Hinsakian Ocean|Great Hinsakian Ocean]] to the east. The continent covers around {{convert|12,671,321|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land, making it the 2nd largest sub-continent in the world, with a total of about 305,354,539 as of 1765, making it also the 7th largest sub-continent by population.
