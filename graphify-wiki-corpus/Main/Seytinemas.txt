'''Seytinemas''', officially the '''Republic of Seytinemas''', is a country in [[West Mahea]]. Situated on around the [[Lanol]] River, it shares land borders with [[Trian]] to the west, [[Sivoso]] and [[Lieri]] to the north, [[Pelugrotoa]] and [[Taing]] to the east, and [[Chaenia]] and [[Dual Cenryia]] to the south. The [[Ubrana Sea]] lies to the east of the mainland. It has a [[List of countries and dependencies by population|population of over 70 million]]. [[Vulna]] is the nation's capital and largest city.

In the 4th to 11th century, the region was a battleground for ancient [[Trians]], [[Huans]], [[Cenryi]], and Seytinemese. Stability came when the [[Seytinemasi Empire]] conquered the region in 1164. After the [[Pashal Elunt]] state splintered, tribal invasions in the region resumed. Around the 11th century, these territories were settled by the [[Early Seytinemes Culture|early Seytinemes]], with the [[Trian Migration|trian minority migrating west]]. It dominated most of the [[West Mahea]] and significantly influenced surrounding cultures by developing the Modern [[Tiaaoan script]]. Under the rule of the [[Oum's dynasty]], the country rose to the status of a mighty empire and great power. The [[Seytinemasi Empire]] lasted until the early 13th century, when [[Sivoso|Sivosan]] emperor [[Dialq the Great]] conquered and dismantled it. A successful [[Seytinemese revolt in 1241]] established a [[Second Seytinemasi Empire]], which reached. After numerous exhausting wars and feudal strife, the empire disintegrated, and in 1244, fell under Sivosan rule again for nearly [[four centuries]].

The [[Trian Independence war|Trian Independence war of 1628-1633]] resulted in the formation of the third and current [[Seytinemese state]], which declared independence from the [[Sivoan Empire]] in 1631, though became an [[Emara|Emaran]] colony in just a few years later in 1634. Many ethnic Seytinemese were left outside the new nation's borders which stoked [[Wikipedia:Irredentism|irredentist]] sentiments that led to several conflicts with its neighbours after independence and alliances with [[Dahe]]. In 1707, Seytinemas came under the [[Estijan|Estijan-led]] Sphere and became a nationalistic state. The ruling [[Nationalistic Party of Seytinemas|Nationalistic Party]] gave up its monopoly on power after the [[Seytinemese Civil War|Civil War]] lasting from 1717-1719. Seytinemas then transitioned into a [[Wikipedia:Democracy|democracy]], and allowed multiparty elections.

Owing to [[Seytinemese economic miracle|record economic growth from 1720 to 1740]], Seytinemas is a [[Wikipedia:Developed_country|developed country]] with an advanced high-income economy: [[Wikipedia:Manufacturing|manufacturing]] and [[Wikipedia:Tourism|tourism]] are major economic sectors, with Seytinemas being the ninth most-visited country in the world in 2024. Seytinemas is part of multiple international organisations and forums. The country's rich historical legacy is reflected partly by its 31 [[Global Heritage Sites|Global Heritage Sites.]]
