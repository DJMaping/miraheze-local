'''Talvani''' is the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital capital] of [[Quidic]], the [[1756 FLLA World Cup|1756 FLLA World Cup]] champions and the twentieth-largest national economy by nominal [[List of countries by GDP (nominal)|GDP]] in the world. The city had a population of 19,281,000 at the [[Quidic|Quidican]] 1764 census, ranking it the [[List of cities and dependencies by population|tenth-largest city in the world]] by population. Talvani is home to the '''Talvani Museum of Sport''', which holds in perpetuity '''The Eserni''', the permanent trophy of the [[1756 FLLA World Cup]] commissioned by the joint [[QUFVL]] hosting consortium of [[Erkizil]], [[Quidic]], [[Wundry]] and [[Ztesh]].
