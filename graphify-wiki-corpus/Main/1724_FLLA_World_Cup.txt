The '''1724 [[FLLA]] World Cup''' was the 14th edition of the [[FLLA World Cup]], the quadrennial international [[Arraby]] tournament contested by the senior men's national teams of the member associations of the [[FLLA]]. It was held in [[Areoix Lie]] across [[Acrola]], and was won by [[Verusa]], who defeated [[Easuhura]] 3-1 in the final.

The 1724 edition was the first FLLA World Cup held in [[Acrola]], hosted by [[Areoix Lie]], and the first to be contested by an expanded field of twenty-four teams under a reformatted group-and-knockout system. The expansion was approved by the FLLA Council in 1721 to extend participation to a further eight associations. [[Verusa]] won their first title with a 3-1 victory over [[Easuhura]] in the final. The trophy itself had been redesigned in advance of the tournament, although the present [[FLLA World Cup#Trophy|FLLA World Cup Trophy]] was not introduced until the [[1740 FLLA World Cup|1740 edition]].
