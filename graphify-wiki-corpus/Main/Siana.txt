'''Siana''', officially the '''Republic of''' '''Siana''', is a country in [[West Ayuma]]. Siana consists of 39 [[Provinces of Siana|provinces]]; it borders [[Darewa]] and [[Lycroa]] to the east, [[Emara]] to the south, [[Merela Sta]], [[Palina]] and [[Verusa]] to the north, and the Rianian Ocean to the west. The official language is Sianan, with [[Darewa|Hadan]] as a secondary official language in the province of [[Hiyamasa]]. People from the Siana are also referred to as Cesen.

''Siana'' literally means "Land of the plains" in reference to its [[Wikipedia:Low_Elevation_Coastal_Zone|low elevation]] and flat topography, with 6% below sea level. In the [[Sianan Republican ear|Republican]] period, which began in 1300s, Siana was a unique era of political, economic, and cultural greatness, ranked among the most powerful and influential in [[Ayuma]] and the world; this period is known as the [[Sianan Golden Age]]. During this time, its traders and early [[Wikipedia:Bank|banks]], established themselves all over the continent and trading posts in major trade hubs around the world.

With a population of over [[List of countries and dependencies by population|39 million people]], all living within a total area of 303,188km<sup>2</sup> (117,061sq mi) Siana is the [[List of countries and dependencies by population density|49th most]] densely populated country, with a density of 129.7 people per square kilometre (335.9people/sq mi). Nevertheless, it is the [[List of largest producing countries of agricultural commodities|world's fourth-largest exporter of food and agricultural products]] by value, owing to its fertile soil, mild climate, [[intensive agriculture]], and inventiveness. The four largest cities in Sinia are [[Wadika]], [[Hapuagri]], [[Tungkam]] and [[Sariyah]]. Wadika is the country's most [[Most Populous citys in Siana|populous city]] and the nominal capital, though the primary political institutions are located in [[Sariyah]].

Siana has been a [[Wikipedia:Parliamentary_republic|parliamentary republic]] with a unitary structure since 1717. The country has a deeply ingrained tradition of [[Wikipedia:Group_polarization|Polarisation]](separation of citizens into groups by religion and political beliefs) and a long record of [[Wikipedia:Toleration|social tolerance]], having legalised [[Wikipedia:Prostitution|prostitution]] and [[Wikipedia:Euthanasia|euthanasia]], along with maintaining a strong [[Wikipedia:Drug|drug]] policy. The Siana allowed [[Wikipedia:Women%27s_suffrage|women's suffrage]] in 1682 and was the third country to legalise [[Wikipedia:Same-sex_marriage|same-sex marriage]] in 1752. Its [[Wikipedia:Mixed_economy|mixed-market advanced economy]] has the [[List of countries by GDP (nominal) per capita|nineteenth-highest per capita]] income globally. The Port of [[Tungkam]] is one busiest in Ayuma. [[Ominiva airport|Ominiva]] is the busiest airport in Siana, and the seventh busiest in Ayuma. Being a [[Wikipedia:Developed_country|developed country]], Siana is a founding member of the Global Trade Union and was readmitted in 1724.
