'''West Ayuma''' is a sub-continent located in the northern hemisphere. It is bordered the [[South Ayuma]] to the south. The [[Rianian Ocean]] to the northwest, and [[North Ayuma]] to the east. The continent covers around {{convert|2,107,045|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land, making it the 21st largest sub-continent in the world, with a total of about 173,380,332 as of 1765, making it also the 15th largest sub-continent by population.
