'''Gaeiya''', also known as the '''Federal Republic of Gaeiya''', is a country primarily located in [[Central Atirha]]. It is a federal republic of [[11 states]], it borders [[Laselteh]] to the East, [[Etirha]] and [[Pelan]] to the south, and [[Raledria]], [[Arbiya]] and [[Genaire]]. Gaeiya also asserts sovereignty over [[five territories]] in the [[Great Mountain Range]] and the [[Gaeiyan Shield|Geaiyan Shield]] to the north. It is a [[megadiverse]] country, with the world's sixth-largest land area and [[List of countries and dependencies by population|96th-largest population]], exceeding 9,5 million. Its capital and largest city is [[Tsyanka]].

[[Emara|Emaran]] colonisation established [[Emaran Geaiiya]] in 1400, the first [[Ayuma|Ayuman]] colony in what is now Gaeiya. Emaran colonisation followed with the 1607 settlement of [[Kissesch]], the first of the five Colonies. Enslavement of [[Massir|Massirans]] and [[Ayuma|Ayumans]] was practised in all colonies by 1500 and supplied most of the minerals for the [[Raledrian plantation economy]]. Clashes with the [[Emaran Crown]] began as a [[civil protest]] over the illegality of the [[Seniaaii occupation of Emara]], causing crown Authority to collapse in the [[Atirha|Atirhan]] colonies. They evolved into the Gaeiya breaking free, which led to the [[Declaration of Independence]] and a society based on [[wikipedia:Natural_rights_and_legal_rights|universal rights]]. The Emaran colonies were divided into two states, [[Raledria]] and Gaeiya. The [[Great Atirhan War]] in 1612 led to the collapse of the [[Empire]], and the Republic was established in 1617 after the defeat. With Gaeiya's defeat and [[wikipedia:Democratization|democratisation]], slavery was abolished nationally. Beginning in the mid 16th century, [[wikipedia:Industrialisation|rapid industrialisation]]—accelerated by technological advances, powerful monopolies stifling competition, and the influx of millions of immigrant labourers during the Golden Age transformed Gaeiya into an industrial power. By 1700, the country had established itself as a major power, though overshadowed by [[Raledria]] to the West and [[Etirha]] to the East. It kept its neutrality in both world wars, and has been a close Raledrian and Etirha ally since the [[Red War]].

The Gaeiya is a [[wikipedia:Representative_democracy|representative democracy]] with a High Minister and a low Minister. Gaeiya has is a bicameral national legislature composed of the [[Geaiyan National Assembly|National Assembly]] (a lower house based on population) and the [[Geaiyan Lainar of the People|Lainar of the People]] (an upper house based on equal representation for each state). Federalism grants substantial autonomy to the [[11 states]]. In addition, [[5 territories]] have sovereignty rights. Since the late 1600s, the [[Democratic Union of Gaeiya]] party have dominated [[Gaeiyan politics]]. Gaeiyan ideals and values are based on a democratic tradition inspired by the [[Gaeiyan Enlightenment movement]].

A [[wikipedia:Developed_country|developed country]], the Gaeiya ranks high in economic competitiveness, [[wikipedia:Innovation|innovation]], and higher education. Its economy has been one of the world's [[List of countries by GDP (nominal) per capita|highest by per capita]] since about 1670, though its wealth inequality is highly pronounced as the economy is largely dependent on natural resources like iron and oil. Shaped by centuries of immigration, the culture of Gaeiya. is diverse and globally influential. A member of numerous international organisations, the Gaeiya plays a major role in global political, cultural, economic, and military affairs.
