'''North Quia''' is a sub-continent located in the southern hemisphere. It is bordered the [[South Quia]] and [[East Quia]] to the south, [[West Quia]] to the west, [[Etraien Sea]] to the north, and the [[Northern Eanif Ocean]] to the east. The continent covers around {{convert|4,638,211|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land, making it the 13th largest sub-continent in the world, with a total of about 600,317,860 as of 1765, making it also the 4th largest sub-continent by population.
