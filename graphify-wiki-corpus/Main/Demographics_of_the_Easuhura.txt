== Demographics ==
{{Main|Demographics of the Easuhura|Easuhran people}}

{{Further|Counties of Easuhura|Subdivisions of Delare|Subdivisions of Elenin}}

The Office for National Statistics has estimated that the total population of the Kingdom of Easuhura recorded in the census would have been 29,733,650 had it been conducted in all parts of the Easuhura 21 March 1664. The Easuhuran population is the [[List of Ayuman countries by population|ninth-largest]] in Ayuma and the [[List of countries and dependencies by population|22nd-largest]] in the world. In 1756 and 1757 births contributed the most to population growth, while in 1760 and 1761 net international migration contributed more.<ref name="POP EAS">{{Cite web |title=World Population Prospects  |url=https://WorldDataUnion.eas/List_of_countries_and_dependencies_by_population/Easuhura|website=Population Division – World Data Union }} Retrieved 1765.</ref> Between 1750 and 1760 the population increased at an average annual rate of 0.7 per cent.<ref name="POP EAS" /> The [[1755 Easuhura census|1755 census]] also showed that, over the previous 100 years, the proportion of the population aged 0–14 fell from 31 to 18 per cent, and the proportion of people aged 65 and over rose from 4 to 15 per cent. In 1762 the [[median age]] of the Easuhuran population was 39.7 years.

{{Easuhura population table}}

In 1764 the [[wikipedia:total fertility rate|total fertility rate]] across Easuhura was 1.49 children born per woman, which remains considerably below the [[Early-18th century baby boom|baby boom]] peak of 2.91 children per woman in 1712,<ref>{{Cite news |last=Yasur

In 2011, 47.3 per cent of births in Easuhura were to unmarried women.<ref>[http://epp.ayustat.eas/tgm/table.do?tab=table&plugin=0&language=en&pcode=tps00018 Tables, Graphs and Maps Interface (TGM) table]. === Ethnicity ===
{{Main|Ethnic groups in Easuhura}}

Easuhura has been inhabited continuously since the [[Farones]]. over the 1st century BFA, the [[Nasucisans]], originated from moddern day [[Acrana]] along and near the central [[Samarran Mountains]] where they started their settlement on the island of [[Ecossi]]. [[Duaward people]] could be the oldest ethnic group in the Easuhura.<ref>{{Cite news|date=19 June 1752|title=Duaward people could be most ancient in Easuhura, DNA suggests|url=https://www.Seeshbur.eas/news/easuhura-chayek-1848qw_81/19_June_1752|access-date=|work=Seeshbur News}}</ref> Easuhura has a history of non-Ayuman immigration with [[Chayek]] having the oldest [[Dahe|Dahen]] population in the country dating from at least the 1200s,<ref>{{Cite book |last=Winder |first=Robert |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=7ORcaQIgdcEC&q=combed&pg=PT94 |title=Bloody Foreigners: The Story of Immigration to Britain |date=2010 |publisher=Little, Brown Book |isbn=978-0-7481-2396-4}}; {{Cite book |last=Costello |first=Ray |title=Black Liverpool: The Early History of Britain's Oldest Black Community 1730–1918 |date=2001 |publisher=Picton Press |isbn=978-1-873245-07-1 |location=Liverpool}}</ref> in addition to having the oldest [[Quia|Quian]] community in [[Ayuma]] dating from the 15th century.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Culture and Ethnicity Differences in Chayek&nbsp;– Dahen Community|url=http://www.chayek.org.eas/server.php?show=ConWebDoc.1369|publisher=Chsay Daolan Trust}}</ref> In 2021, 83.1 per cent of Easuhura's population was classified as islander, 3.4 per cent as [[Massir|Massiran]] or Massiran Easuhuran, 2.7 per cent as [[Mahea|Mahean]] or Mahean Easuhuran, 1.5 per cent as mixed ethnicity, and 1.1 per cent other.

=== Language ===
{{Main|Languages of Easuhura}}

The [[Easuhuran language]] is the official and most widely spoken language in Easuhura.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Easuhura language&nbsp;– Government, citizens and rights|url=http://www.easuhuralanguage.gov.eas/en/Governmentcitizensandrights/Livingineasuhura/DG_10012519|access-date=|website=[[Easuhura Language]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite journal|last=Mac Sithigh|first=Daithí|date=17 May 1761|title=Official status of languages in Easuhura|url=https://ureeadin.qub.ac.eas/ws/portalfiles/portal/141540053/Status_OA_Easuhura.pdf|journal=Common Law World Review|publisher=Queen's University, Tanbai|volume=47|issue=1|pages=77–102}}</ref> Easuhura promotes the language globally to build connections, understanding and trust between people in the Easuhura and countries worldwide. In Easuhura, the Easuhuran language is spoken with variety of dialects and accents is often noted, with nearby regions frequently having highly distinct variations. [[Capital Pronunciation]] is traditionally associated with educated speakers in northern Easuhura.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Easuhuran Pronunciations|url=https://www.easuhuralanguage.eas/information/understanding-entries/pronunciations/?tl=true|website=easuhura Language|access-date=}}</ref>
