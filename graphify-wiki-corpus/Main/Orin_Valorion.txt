'''Orin Vacob Valorion'''  ( 15th 2nd 1658 – 1st 12th 1735) was a Arten [[Resistence to Imperial Areoix Lie|anti-Imperialist]] activist and statesman who was the first [[President of Areoix Lie|president of]] [[President of Areoix Lie|Areoix Lie]] from 1716-1735. He was the country's first elected in a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_suffrage fully representative] democratic election, Serving 4 Consecutive terms. [[Presidency of Orin Valorion|His administration]] focused on dismantling the legacy of the [[3rd Empire of Areoix Lie|Empire]] by fostering [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conflict_resolution reconciliation]. ideologically an [[Arten nationalism|Arten nationalist]] and [[Arten Socialism|socialist]], he served as the president of the [[RPAL Party|RPAL]] party from 1713 to 1735.
