'''South Acrola''' is a sub-continent located in the Northern and Southern hemispheres. It is bordered the [[Middle Acrola]] and [[North Acrola]] to the north, the [[Diairesi Sea]] to the west, the [[Mahea]] to the north, and the [[Hinsakian Ocean|Great Hinsakian Ocean]] to the east. The continent covers around {{convert|5,821,227|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land, making it the 8th largest sub-continent in the world, with a total of about 263,489,404 as of 1765, making it also the 10th largest sub-continent by population..
