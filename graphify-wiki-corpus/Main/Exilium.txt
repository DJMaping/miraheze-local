'''Exilium''', formally the '''Exilium Republic''', is a country located in [[South Mahea]]. It borders [[Dual Cenryia]] north, the [[Trayd Cren sea]] to the west, and [[Inrea]] to the south. At [[List of countries and dependencies by area|452,426 square kilometres (174,683 sq mi)]], Exilium is the largest [[Folite country]] by both area and population, and is the twelfth-largest country. Its capital and largest city is [[Dulvasra]]. Exilium has a [[List of countries and dependencies by population|population of 25.8 million], and a low population density of [[List of countries and dependencies by population density|55.4 inhabitants per square kilometre (143.5/sq mi)]]; 76% of Exilians reside in [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_area urban areas]]. They are mostly in the central and western half of the country. Exilium's urban areas together cover 2.5% of its land area. Exilium has a [[Exilium climate|diverse climate]].

Exilium has been inhabited since [[prehistoric times]], around 35,000 BFA. The inhabitants emerged as the [[Landulsar peoples]], later the [[Dralu peoples|Dralu]] and [[Eru’a peoples|Eru’a]], who formed part of the sea-faring peoples known as the [[Folites peoples|Folites]]. A unified Exilian state was established during the late 9th century. In 1397, [[Caa Non]] joined Exilium. When Exilium became involved in the decision of [[Dual Cenryia]] on the [[Dual Cenryia|Cenri]] and later [[Sivoso|Sivosan]] Side, an expansion of its territories began, forming the [[Exilian Empire]], which remained one of the great powers of [[Mahea]] until the early 15th century. During this era, Exilium controlled much of the southern [[Otemvi]]. The last war in which Exilium was directly involved was in 1643, when Exilium, by military means, forced [[Easuhura]] not become a protectorate. The [[Easuhura|Easuhuran]] [[protectorate]] only lasted 3 years. Exilium underwent [[wikipedia:Democratization|Democratization]] in 1728 AFA after the king stepped down due to [[major protests]] from the worsening economy.  

Exilium is a [[wikipedia:Developing_country|developing country]] that is classified as having a lower-middle-income economy. Its main local trading partner is [[Dual Cenryia]]; to ensure economic stability, Exilium's currency is the [[Exilian Iscali]]. Exilium's major overseas trading partners are the [[Pelugrotoa]] and [[Dahe]]. The majority of the country's employment is provided by its [[wikipedia:Agriculture|agricultural]] and [[wikipedia:Manufacturing|manufacturing sectors]]. Exilium is a member of the [[Mahen Development Community]].
