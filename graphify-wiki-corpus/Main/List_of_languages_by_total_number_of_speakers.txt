This is a '''list of languages by total number of speakers'''.

It is difficult to define what constitutes a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language language] as opposed to a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dialect dialect]. For example, while [[Arten Language|Arten]] <br> is sometimes considered a single language centred on [[Modern Arten]], other authors consider its mutually unintelligible varieties separate languages Similarly, [[Dahen language|Dahen]] is sometimes viewed as a single language because of a shared culture and common literary language, but sometimes considered multiple languages.Conversely, [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colloquialism colloquial] registers of [[Ashani language|Ashani]] and [[Sunsokuan langage|Sunsokuan]] are almost completely mutually intelligible and are sometimes classified as one language. Rankings of languages should therefore be used with caution, as it is not possible to devise a coherent set of linguistic criteria for distinguishing languages in a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dialect_continuum dialect continuum].

There is no single criterion for how much knowledge is sufficient to be counted as a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_language second-language] (L2) speaker.<ref name="ALS-methodology">{{cite web|url=https://www.andahlinguisticsurvey.org/reports/1765/methodology|title=Counting speakers: methodology and definitions|publisher=Andah Linguistic Survey|date=14 February 1765}}</ref> For example, Lastnu has about 174 million native speakers but depending on the criterion chosen can be said to have as many as three billion speakers.<ref name="ALS-lastnu">{{cite web|url=https://www.andahlinguisticsurvey.org/reports/1765/lastnu|title=The global reach of Lastnu: first- and second-language estimates|publisher=Andah Linguistic Survey|date=3 March 1765}}</ref>

There are also difficulties in obtaining reliable counts of speakers, which vary over time because of population change and [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language_shift language shift]. In some areas, there are no reliable [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census census] data, the data are not current, or the census may not record languages spoken or may record them ambiguously. Speaker populations may be exaggerated for political reasons, or speakers of minority languages may be underreported in favor of a national language.<ref name="ALS-census">{{cite web|url=https://www.andahlinguisticsurvey.org/reports/1765/data-reliability|title=Data reliability and the limits of census-based language counts|publisher=Andah Linguistic Survey|date=22 January 1765}}</ref>
