The '''Blue River''' is one of the [[List of rivers of Ayuma#Rivers of Ayumaby length|longest river]] in [[Ayuma]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.worldatlas.andah.com/rivers/10-longest-rivers-in-ayuma.html|title=10 Longest Rivers In Ayuma}}</ref> Situated in [[Estijan]], it flows through [[Central Estijan]] to [[northern Estijan]] and into the [[Sarwun Strait]] and [[Rianian Ocean]]. The Blue River has a length of {{cvt|2531|km|mi}}, and a catchment area of {{cvt|1336500|km2}}.<ref name="gvr">[https://rivers.est/gvr/index.php?card=179058], Estijan State Water Registry</ref> It is also Ayumas second largest river in terms of average [[Wikipedia:discharge_(hydrology)|discharge]] at delta – between {{convert|8,000|and|8500|m3|ft3}} per second – and of [[wikipedia:drainage_basin|drainage basin]]. It is widely regarded as the [[Rivers in Estijan|national river]] of [[Estijan]]. The old [[Yirvan States]], arose along the Blue and neibouring rivers 600 AFA.<ref name="Koabolm">{{Cite web|last=Koabolm|first=Tore|title=Birka, River Emporium|url=https://www.academia.edu.est/40313672/372}}</ref> Historically, the river served as an important meeting place of various [[Ayuma|Ayuman]] civilizations.<ref>{{Cite book |title=Grand Strategy of the Huan |last=Luttwak, Ertuard N. |date=1751 |publisher=Belknap Sivoso |pages=52}}</ref><ref>{{Cite journal |last=Walker |first=Joel |date=1749 |title=Estijan and Its Neighbors in Late Antiquity: Art of the Huan |journal=Pelugrotoan Journal of Archaeology |volume=1 11 |issue=4 |pages=797}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book |title=The Blue River river |last=McNeese |first=Tim |date=1752 |publisher=Jungui House Publisher |location=Jungui |pages=14–16}}</ref>

The river flows in Estijan through [[wikipedia:forest|forest]]s, [[wikipedia:forest|forest stepps]] and steppes. Five of the [[List of cities and towns in Estijan by population|ten largest cities of Estijan]], including the nation's capital, [[Shabiran]], are located in the Blue River's drainage basin. Because the Blue River drains into the [[Rianian Ocean]].

Some of the largest [[wikipedia:reservoir|reservoir]]s in the world are located along the Blue River. The river has a symbolic meaning in [[Estijan culture]].

==Name==
The Estijan [[wikipedia:hydronym|hydronym]] derives from [[Proto-Yvian]] Blue River which is preserved in many Surtian languages.

==Description==
The Blue River is the one of the longest [[wikipedia:river|river]]s in [[Ayuma]], and its catchment area is entirely inside [[Estijan]], though the longest river in Estijan is the [[Tusil River|Tusil]] river.<ref name="readersnatural">{{Cite book |title=Natural Wonders of the World |publisher=Reader's Digest Association, Inc |year=1730 |editor-last=Effel |editor-first=Yihard L. |location=Raledria |pages=406 |editor-last2=Wernet |editor-first2=Susn J.}}</ref> The source of the Blue River lies in the village of [[Tolgohgha]] near the city of [[Tibittarausk]]. Rising in the [[Uialier Hills]] {{convert|345|m|ft|abbr=on}} [[Wikipedia:Height_above_mean_sea_level|above sea level]] southwest of [[Quzhaeil]], the Blue River heads east past [[Lake Rerasaa]], leading to the capital of [[Shabiran]], it the turns towards the north, passing through the cities of [[Barashir]], [[Damiran]], [[Bashira]], [[Ghalnari]] and [[Hananli]]. It discharges into the [[Rianian Ocean]] above [[Sarwun]] the second largest city in [[Estijan]].<ref name="readersnatural" />

The Blue River has many [[tributaries]], most importantly the [[Bermli (river)|Bermli]], the [[Sters River|Sters]], the [[Lufah (river)|Lufah]], and the [[Fah (river)|Fah]]. The Blue River and its tributaries form the Blue River river system, which flows through an area of about {{convert|1336500|km2|abbr=on}} in the most heavily populated part of Estijan.<ref name="readersnatural" /> 

The Blue River drains most of [[Western Estijan]]. Its many large reservoirs provide [[wikipedia:irrigation|irrigation]] and [[wikipedia:hydroelectric|hydroelectric]] power. The [[Shabiran Canal]], the [[Blue River–Bajam Canal]], and the [[Blue-Tusil River Waterway]] form navigable [[wikipedia:waterway|waterway]]s connecting the Blue river and Tusil River to the [[Tasa Bay]]. High levels of chemical [[Wikipedia:pollution_pollution]] have adversely affected the river and its habitats, oranaging from the heavy levels of mining in the Amnant Mountains,.

The fertile river valley provides large quantities of [[wikipedia:wheat|grains]] and other agricultural produce, and also has many mineral riches. A substantial natural gas industry centres on the Blue River valley. Other resources include [[wikipedia:salt|salt]], and [[wikipedia:potash|potash]]. The Blue River Delta and the [[Sarwun Strait]] are [[wikipedia:fishing grounds|fishing grounds]].

==Navigation==
The Blue River is of great importance to inland shipping and transport in Estijan: all the dams in the river have been equipped with large (double) [[Canal lock|ship locks]], so that vessels of considerable dimensions can travel almost to the upstream end of the river. Connections with the [[Tusil River]] are possible through the [[Blue-Tusil River Waterwayl]].
