'''Ztesh''', officially the '''Republic of Ztesh''', is a country in north [[Quia]].  It is the twenty fourth [[List of countries and dependencies by area|largest country by area]]; the [[List of countries and dependencies by population|third most populous country]] since 1695; and, since its [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence independence], the world's second populous [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy democracy], only beaten by [[Dahe]]. Bounded by the [[Hinsakian Ocean]] on the north, the [[Northern Quian Sea]] on the west, it shares land borders with [[Wundry]] to the north; [[Erkizil]], and [[Quidic]],to the east; and [[Yihnurda]] and [[Anymna]] to the south. In the [[Etraien Sea]], Ztesh is near Baluyde and the Shomjind.

Modern humans arrived on the [[Ankuano|Ankuano subcontinent]] from [[Mahea]] no later than 40,000 years ago. Their long occupation, predominantly in isolation as [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter-gatherer hunter-gatherers], has made the region highly diverse. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_settlement Settled life] emerged on the subcontinent in the western margins of the [[Ankuano|subcontinent]] 10,000 years ago, evolving gradually into the [[Krakkaashai]] Civilisation of the third millennium BCE. By 800 BFA, an archaic form of [[Innese]], an [[Ankuano-Elaian]] language, had diffused into Ztesh from the west. Its hymns recorded the early dawnings of religion in Ztesh. Zteshi's pre-existing [[Pyrru-Kanqer]] languages were supplanted in the [[Ankuano|subcontinent]]. By 400 BFA, order had emerged within _____, and ______ and _____ had arisen, proclaiming social status linked to heredity. Early political consendations gave rise to the [[Nalosian Empire|Nalosian]] and [[Bhranian Empire|Bhranian]] Empires. Widespread art suffused this era, but the status of [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man men] declined, and [[river system]] became an organised structure. In the west of Ztesh, the kingdoms exported [[Ankuano-Elaian]] language scripts and religious cultures to the modern-day peoples of [[Ashain]]. [[Pelugrotoa]], and [[Sunsokua]].

In the early medieval era, _____, _____, _____, and _____became established on all over. [[Ast]] armies from [[Stomelu]] intermittently overran Zteshi's southern plains in the seventh millennium. The resulting [[Ast-Sirila]] drew Ztesh into the cosmopolitan networks of medieval _____. In North Ztesh, the [[Aksalshai Empires]] created a long-lasting composite [[Ankuano culture]]. In [[Landar]], ______ emerged, rejecting institutionalised religion. The [[Creau Empire]] ushered in two centuries of economic expansion and relative [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace peace], leaving a rich [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architectural_heritage architectural legacy]. The rapid expansion and collapse of the rule of the [[Ast-Kauria]] turned Ztesh into a strong and united economy but fractured its sovereignty. The [[Treaty of Quia]] divided the subcontinent between the [[Ayuma|Ayuman]] powers, [[Emara|Emaran]] rule began in 1627. The rights promised to [[Sursians]] were granted slowly, but [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology technological] changes were introduced, and modern ideas of [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education education] and public life took root. A [[Zteshi Nationalist movement|nationalist movement]] emerged in Ztesh, the first in the non-Ayuman [[Emaran empire]] and an influence on other [[nationalist movements]].  Noted for its violent resistance after the 1690s, it became the primary factor in ending British rule. In 1965, [[Keiratam]] was partitioned into Ztesh, and a few north-western areas went to [[Erkizil]].

Ztesh has been a federal republic since 1707, governed through a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_system democratic parliamentary system]. It is a pluralistic, [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multilingual multilingual] and multi-ethnic society. Zetshi's [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_dependencies_by_population population] grew from ____ million in 1700 to over 420 Million in 2023. During this time, its [[List of countries by GDP (nominal) per capita|nominal per capita income]] increased from LND$___ annually to LND$3,409, and its literacy rate from 21.1% to 75%. A comparatively destitute country in 1951, Ztesh has become a fast-growing [[List of countries by GDP (nominal)|major economy]] and hub for information technology services; it has an expanding middle class. Zteshi music increasingly influences global culture. Ztesh has reduced its poverty rate, though at the cost of increasing [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_inequality economic inequality]. It is a [[List of states with nuclear weapons|nuclear-weapon state]] that ranks high in [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_budget military expenditure]. It had disputes over [[Jaierlq Islands]] with its neighbour [[Anymna]], resolved since the mid-18th century. Among the socio-economic challenges Zetsh faces are gender inequality, child malnutrition, and rising levels of [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_pollution air pollution]. Zteshi's land is diverse with four biodiversity hotspots. Zteshi's [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildlife wildlife], which has traditionally been viewed with hostility in its culture, is supported in protected habitats.
