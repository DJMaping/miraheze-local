'''Albiran''' is the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital capital] of [[Etirha]], an [[Atirha|Atirhan]] economy and the sixth-largest national economy by nominal [[List of countries by GDP (nominal)|GDP]] in the world. The city had a population of 10,119,000 at the [[Etirha|Etirhan]] 1764 census, ranking it the [[List of cities and dependencies by population|thirty-second-largest city in the world]] by population. Albiran is the political and cultural centre of the [[Etirha|Etirhan]] state and was the host city of the [[1720 FLLA World Cup]], the first FLLA World Cup contested in [[Etirha]].
