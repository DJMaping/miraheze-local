The ''' Fire Coast''' is a country in [[South Acrola]]. It is situated next to the [[Hinsakian Ocean]] to the east, and shares borders with [[Pelines]] to the northwest, [[Rijan Bu]] and [[Deschon]] to the south. The Fire Coast covers an [[List of countries and dependencies by area|area of 99,744 km2 (38,511 sq mi)]], spanning diverse [[ecologies]], from coastal savannas to [[wikipedia:Tropical_rainforest|tropical rainforests]]. With over [[List of countries and dependencies by population|7.1 million inhabitants]], the Fire Coast is the [[List of countries and dependencies by population|ninetenth-most populous country in Acrola]], and the seventh-most populous country in South Acrola specifically. The capital and largest city is [[Kamoraya]].

Beginning in the late 16th century, the [[Kuwana Dynasty]], followed by the [[Ayuma|Ayuman powers,]] contested the area for trading rights until the Ashani ultimately established [[control of the coast]] by the early 16th century. Following more than a century of [[colonial resistance]], the country's current borders took shape, encompassing four separate [[Ashani colonial territories]]: Fire Coast, the Ashanti, the Southern Territories, and Ashani Acroland. These were unified as a single colony after the [[Red War]] under [[Emara]]. On 6 March 1710, the Fire Coast became the first colony in [[South Acrola]] to achieve sovereignty. Under [[President Kwaumah]], it became influential in decolonisation efforts and the [[Pan-Acrolan movement]].

The Fire Coast is a multi-ethnic country with diverse linguistic and religious groups; while the [[Retui]] are the [[largest ethnic group]], they constitute a plurality. Most of the Fyrans are _____(71.3%); almost a fifth are _____; a tenth practise traditional faiths or report no religion. The Fire Coast is a [[wikipedia:Unitary_parliamentary_republic|unitary semi-presidential republic]] led by a president who is head of state. For political stability in [[Acrola]], the Fire Coast ranked seventh in the 1763 [[Rahim Index of Acrolan Governance]]. Since 1743, it has maintained one of the freest and most stable governments on the continent, and performs relatively well in healthcare, economic growth, and human development. It has a significant influence on S[[outh Acrola]] and [[Acrola]] as a whole. The Fire Coast is highly integrated in international affairs, being a founding member of the [[Acrolan Union]], and a member of the [[Global Trade Union]], [[Democratic Action Council]], and [[South Acrolan Cooperation Council]].
