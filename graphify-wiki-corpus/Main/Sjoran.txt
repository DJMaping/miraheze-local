'''Sjoran''' is the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital capital] of [[Ashain]], a [[Quia|Quian]] economy and the fifteenth-largest national economy by nominal [[List of countries by GDP (nominal)|GDP]] in the world. The city had a population of 14,751,000 at the [[Ashain|Ashainian]] 1764 census, ranking it the [[List of cities and dependencies by population|seventeenth-largest city in the world]] by population. [[Ashain]] has been confirmed as a co-host of the [[1776 FLLA World Cup]] under the joint [[QUFVL]] hosting arrangement with [[Ahokini]].
