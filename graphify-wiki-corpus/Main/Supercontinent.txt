The '''Supercontinent''' is a collection of continents located across the northern and southern hemispheres. It includes [[Ayuma]] and [[Acrola]] in the north, and [[Mahea]] and [[Massir]] to the south, The supercontinent covers around {{convert|69,729,927|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land and with a total of about population of 4,454,200,000 as of 1765, making up around 65% of the global population.
