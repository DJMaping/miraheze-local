'''West Quia''' is a sub-continent located in the southern hemisphere. It is bordered the [[Hinsakian Ocean]] to the north, [[Haanian Ocean]] to the southwest, and the [[North Quia]] to the east. The continent covers around {{convert|2,970,040|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land, making it the 18th largest sub-continent in the world, with a total of about 371,199,741 as of 1765, making it also the 5th largest sub-continent by population.
