'''East Mahea''' synonymous with '''[[Hijita]]''' is a sub-continent located in the southern hemisphere. It is bordered the [[Haanian Ocean]]  to the south and east, the [[Adrad Sea]], [[Praosia Sea]] and [[Gulf of Ale]] to the west, and the [[Hinsakian Ocean|Great Hinsakian Ocean]] to the north. The continent covers around {{convert|1,708,765|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land, making it the 23rd largest sub-continent in the world, with a total of about 203,928,833 as of 1765, making it also the 13th largest sub-continent by population.
