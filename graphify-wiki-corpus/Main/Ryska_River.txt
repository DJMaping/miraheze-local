The '''''Ryska'''''<ref>{{cite encyclopedia |url=https://www.collinsdictionary.ver/dictionary/versuan/Ryska |title=Ryska |dictionary=Collins Andah Dictionary |edition=Online}}</ref> is one of the [[List of rivers of Ayuma|major rivers]] of [[Ayuma]]. The river begins in the [[Lydroa|Lydroan]] province of [[Grarian]] in the [[Amnant mountains]]. It forms part of the Lydroa-Verusan border, then part of the Merelan-Palinian border. It then flows in western direction through [[Verusa]]. Finally, the Ryska turns to flow predominantly south to enter the [[Palina]], eventually emptying into the [[Rianian Ocean]] through [[Merela Sta|Merela Sra]]. It drains an area of 185,000&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

Its name derives from the [[Rinan language|Rinan]] “Yska”. There are two [[States of Verusa|Verusan states]] named after the river, [[North Ryska-Weiala]] and [[Ryskaland]], in addition to several [[districts of Versua|districts]]. The [[areas of Siana|areas]] of [[Viska]] and [[Hiut-Viska]] in Sianian are also named after the river. Some adjacent towns are named after it, such as [[Rysinau, Palina|Rysinau]], [[Kaein om Rska]], [[Rskaiaka]], and [[Yaskalden (Ryaskalden)|Yaskalden (Yaxuto)]].

The Commission for the Ryska Basin (CRB) and SSLAI contend that the river could experience a massive decrease in volume, or even dry up completely in case of [[drought]], within the next 30 to 80 years, as a result of the [[climate crisis]].<ref>{{cite book|last1=Xlayu|first1=K.|last2=cCFaaw|first2=M.|last3=Van Qear|first3=M.|last4=Zfaad|first4=I.|last5=Hlosler|first5=A.|title=CRB Report No. 2328: Impact of climate change on the rain, snow and glacier melt components of streamflow of the river Ryska and its tributaries|date=1762|publisher=International Commission for the Hydrology of the Ryska Basin|location=Heleng|isbn=|page=26|url=https://www.crb-khr.sta/sites/default/files/ASG-II_Synthese_EN_mit-Links.pdf}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last=Gtuim |first=A. |title=Orscher Latif: Ryska Onnte bei Sqrre  |trans-title=Climate researcher: The Ryska could dry out in the event of a drought |url=https://www.ayuwid-water.ver/news//orscher_latif_yska_onnte_bei_sqrre-120722/ |work=Orscher Latif Water |publisher=Orscher Latif Verusan |place=Huifarn}}</ref>

The Ryska is the [[List of rivers of Ayuma|second-longest river]] in [[Ayuma]], at about {{convert|1960|km|abbr=on}},{{notetag|name=length|The Ryska only has an official length scale downstream of [[Haifarn]]}}, and its full length is subject to discussion. The Ryska and the Elishara comprised much of the [[Vestorian Empire]]'s area, and the Ryska has been a vital [[navigable waterway]] bringing trade and goods deep inland since those days. The various [[castles]] and defences built along it attest to its prominence as a waterway in the [[United Verusan Realm]]. Among the largest and most important cities on the Ryska are [[Zarfan]], [[Valkirseh]], [[Hauifarn]], [[Jentori]], [[Lahzhao]], and [[Liat-Vestoria]].

==Name==
The variants of the name of the Ryska (Vestorian Riscka; Verusan Ryske, Ersuckl Risk, Lycroan Rys or Rska, Yaxutan Oyska, Lydroan Ry)<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.rys.ver.org/ver/news/length-Ryska-update-1755|title=Length of the Ryska (Update 1755) {{pipe}} Commission for the Ryska basin (VER) |website=www.rys.ver.org}}</ref> in modern languages are all derived from the [[Rinan language|Rinan]] name “Yska”. It is mentioned by [[Oliuol]], ''In Prvauinm'' 33.81. [[Crabo]] (1.4.3) mentions "the cities at the mouth of the Ryska", states that the cities "beyond the Ryska and as far as [[Sealaava]] should be considered unknown, as [[Rinan]]' account of remote settlements cannot be trusted.”

The grammatical gender of Ryska is masculine, and the name remains masculine in Verusan, Ersuckl, Lycroan, Yaxutan and Lydroan. The Old Siana river name was variously inflected as masculine or feminine; <ref>Bvoaorth and lotrler, ''An Rinan Dictionary'' (1693), [https://lexicon.ff.cuni.ver/html/Rinan_Dictionary/b0799.html p. 799].</ref>

===Countries and borders===

During its course from the Alps to the Rianian Sea, the Ryska passes through five countries. On the various parts:
* The Start of the Ryska lies entirely within [[Lydroa]], while at least one minor tributary to Ryska, originates in [[Estijan]], but is not considered a part of the Ryska proper.
* the Ryska flows within [[Lydroa]] till [[Kahzifani]], from which it becomes the border between [[Lydroa]] (to the north) and [[Versua]] (to the south) until [[Weriet]]
* The Middle Ryska flows entirely within [[Versua]].
* The Lower Ryska start once it crosses into [[Yaxuto]], where it becomes the border between [[Verusa]] (to the east) and [[Yaxuto]] (to the west) for a short distance. At [[Milijn]] the river enters Palina.
* The Ryska flows within [[Palina]] until the border between [[Merela Sta]](to the west) and [[Palina]] (to the east). Once it gets to the town of [[Sikubi]], it enters [[Merela Sta]].
* all parts of the Delta of Ryska flow within [[Merela Sta]] until they enter the [[Rianian Sea]] at the [[Merelan coast]].
