'''North Mahea''' is a sub-continent located in the southern hemisphere. It is bordered the [[South Mahea]] to the south. [[West Mahea]] to the west, the [[South Acrola]] to the north, and the [[Hinsakian Ocean|Great Hinsakian Ocean]] and [[Adrad Sea]] to the east. The continent covers around {{convert|6,546,180|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land, making it the 6th largest sub-continent in the world, with a total of about 638,348,513 as of 1765, making it also the 3rd largest sub-continent by population.
