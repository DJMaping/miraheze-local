'''Dy''' is a city in [[Ukhdari]] in central [[Ayuma]], with a population of approximately 1.85 million. It was one of nine cities in the country selected as a host city for the [[1760 FLLA World Cup]].

The city's principal sporting venue is the Dy Sports Complex, a 58,700-seat stadium that hosted the third-place playoff of the 1760 tournament, in which [[United Delet]] defeated [[Easuhura]] 3-0 in a penalty shoot-out after a goalless draw. The Sports Complex was substantially redeveloped during the bidding period in advance of the tournament.
