'''Fermori''', officially the '''Republic of Fermori''', is a country in [[North Ayuma]]. It is bordered to the northwest by the [[Rianian Ocean]], to the south by [[Verusa]] and [[Uularin]], and to the east by [[Estijan]]. Its capital city of [[Khalviro]], along with the city of [[Zahriq]], are the country's two largest urban areas. The [[Fermorian language]], of the [[Surtan language family]], is the official language and the first language of the majority of nearly [[List of countries and dependencies by population|12.2 million people]]. 

Present-day Fermori has been inhabited since at least 35,000 BC. The medieval indigenous population of Fermori was one of the last pagan civilisations in Ayuma to adopt _____ following the _____ in the 11th century. After centuries of foreign rule by the [[UVR vassalage|UVR]] and later the [[Zente of Fermori]] in 1564, and [[Estijan Huan]], a distinct [[Fermorian national identity|Fermorian]] [[Fermorian national identity|national identity]] gained new momentum in the mid-16th century. This culminated in the 1733 [[Fermorian Declaration of Independence|Fermorian]] [[Fermorian Declaration of Independence|Declaration of Independence]]. Fermori declared neutrality, but the country was invaded and occupied by the Huan, who did not consent to their independence. Throughout the [[de facto Huan occupation]], Fermori's de jure state continuity was preserved by diplomatic representatives and the [[government-in-exile]]. Following the [[Collapse of the Huan|1739–1740 collapse of the Huan,]] full independence was restored on new years day 1740

Fermori is a [[wikipedia:Developed_country|developed country]] with a [[wikipedia:Developed_country|high-income]], advanced economy,. It is a democratic [[wikipedia:Unitary_parliamentary_republic|unitary parliamentary republic]], with a single-tier local government system consisting of [[43 municipalities]]. Fermori is among the [[least corrupt countries]] in the world and ranks very highly in international rankings for education,[[List of countries by Krali Total Development Index|human development]], press freedom, online public services, and the prevalence of [[technology companies]].
