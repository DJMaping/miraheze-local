'''Eldavpir''', officially the '''Republic of Eldavpir''', is a country on the eastern coast of [[Middle Acrola]]. It is bordered by [[Viussi]] and [[Sattle]] to the south, the [[Iquir]] and [[Terressin]] to the west, [[Umendel]] to the north, and [[Reerica]] and the [[Hoscoara Gulf]] to the east. The capital and most populous city is [[Lunfrix]].

Eldavpir has been inhabited since the [[wikipedia:Paleolithic|Palaeolithic Age]]. After the [[Amarran expansion]] reached the region, states were formed by the 13th century and organised into confederations. The [[Kingdom of Shiqbi]] ascended to achieve hegemony among the other kingdoms from the 14th century. [[Ashain|Ashani]] and [[Estijan]] explorers established relations with [[Shiqbi]] in 1522. The [[Lycroa|Lycroans]] began colonising the coast in the 17th century, creating the colonies of [[Awheake]] and [[Pisareh]]. Shiqbi [[fought three wars]] against the Lycroans, ending in [[the Lycroan conquest of Shiqbi]]. The banning of the [[wikipedia:Slave_trade|slave trade]] in the 17th century disrupted Shiqbi’s undiversified economic system, and Ayuman settlers gradually began to establish their presence in the interior of the region. After the [[Great Imperial War]], the colonies were granted to [[Estijan Huan]] after the [[Treaty of Fraud]].

After a protracted [[anti-colonial struggle]] (1692–1713), Eldavpir achieved independence in 1715 as a [[wikipedia:One-party_state|one-party Republic]], but the country descended into a devastating civil war the same year, between the ruling [[People's Movement for the Liberation of Eldavpir]] (MPLE), backed by the [[Estijan]] and [[Areoix Lie]]; the insurgent [[National Eldavpir Movement]], an originally nationalist and later anti-nationalist group supported by the [[Raledria]] and [[Ashain]]; the militant [[National Liberation Front]], backed by [[Iquir]]. 

Since the end of the civil war after the [[Terst revolution]] in 1723, Eldavpir has emerged as a relatively unstable [[wikipedia:Republic|constitutional republic]], and its economy is among the [[fastest-growing in the world]], with [[Dahe]], [[Raledria]], and [[Areoix Lie]] being the country's largest investment and trade partners. However, the economic growth is highly uneven, with most of the nation's wealth concentrated in a disproportionately small part of the population, as most Eldavpirns have a [[low standard of living; life expectancy]] is among the lowest in the world, while [[wikipedia:Infant_mortality|infant mortality]] is among the highest.
