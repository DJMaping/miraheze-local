'''South Mahea''' is a sub-continent located in the southern hemisphere. It is bordered the [[Haanian Ocean]] to the south, the [[Ubrana Sea]] and [[Trayd Cren Sea]] to the west, [[North Mahea|North]] and [[West Mahea]] to the north, and the [[Adrad Sea]], [[Gulf of Ale]] and the [[Praosia Sea]] to the east. The continent covers around {{convert|5,102,688|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land, making it the 11th largest sub-continent in the world, with a total of about 298,186,865 as of 1765, making it also the 8th largest sub-continent by population behind.
