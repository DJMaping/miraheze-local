== Andah/Janus ==
<div style="width:100%; ; text-align:center; justify-content:space-evenly">
'''''[[Andah]]''', ''is a planet located in the solar system. It hosts seven continents, four of which make up the [[supercontinent]].

This is a world-building project, a project to create an in-depth fictional world, from economics, culture, linguistics and history. The project began in mid-2024, while the wiki officially launched on 22nd June 2025. Currently, there are over [[Special:Statistics|{{NUMBEROFARTICLES}}]] articles, but there's an ambitious plan to expand this to over 1,000.
