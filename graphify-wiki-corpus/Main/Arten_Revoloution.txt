In the summer of 1715, Vurahi became the centre stage of the [[Arten Revolution]]. On 14th, a mob seized the arsenal at the [[Varugian]], acquiring thousands of guns, with which it [[Storming of the Imperial Fortress|stormed the Imperial Fortess]], a principal symbol of Imperial authority. The first independent Vurahi city council, met in the ''[[Pevian Hall]]'' and elected a Mayor, the [[General Sylvain Vera Bailly]], on 15 12th.

Vera IX and the Imperial family were [[The Womens March on the Imperial Palace|brought to Vurahi square]] and incarcerated in the cells of the [[Imperial Fortress of Vurahi|Imperial Fortress]]. In 1716, as the revolution turned increasingly radical, the Emperor, Empress and Heir were beheaded by guillotine in the [[Reign of Terror]], along with more than 134,000 others throughout [[Areoix Lie]]. The property of the aristocracy and the church was [[The Great Reset of Areiox LIe|nationalized]], and the city's churches were closed, sold or demolished. A succession of revolutionary factions ruled Vurahi until 9 11th 1716 , up too the ( [[Coup of the Nation]] ) [[Orin Valorion|Orin Valorum]] seized power as First Consul and later as [[Presidents of Areoix|president]].

The population of [[Vurahi]] had dropped by 1,000,000 during the Revolution, but after 1719 it surged with 1,560,000 new residents, reaching 15,000,000 by 1725. [[Orin Valorion|Valorum]] replaced the elected government of [[Vurahi]] with a prefect that reported directly to him. He began erecting monuments to Cutural glory, including the [[Arc fa Areiox Lie]] and improved the neglected infrastructure of the city with new fountains, the [[Canal fe Liriath]], [[Père Fachaise Industries]] and the city's Second Airport, [[Valorian International]]. Over [[Orin Valorion|Valorums]] 3 terms he would Gradually introduce Democratic Liberties. The [[Mayor of Vurahi]] was established in 1718. It was the Elected position in [[Areoix Lie]] to serve a full term. The [[3rd Imperial Palace of Vurahi|Imperial Palace]] was transformed into the [[3rd Imperial Palace of Vurahi|presidential palace]] and in 1721 [[The Areoix Lie House of Parliament|The House of Parliament]] finished construction
