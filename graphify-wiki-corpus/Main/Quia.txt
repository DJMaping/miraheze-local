'''Quia''' is a continent located in the southern hemisphere. It is bordered by the [[Hinsakian Ocean]] and [[Northern Eanif Ocean]] to the north, the [[Haainian Ocean]] to the west and south, [[Southern Eanif Ocean]] to the east. The continent covers around {{convert|29,918,641|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land, making it the largest continent in the world, with a total of about 1,168,890,000 as of 1765, making it also the 3rd largest continent by population behind [[Mahea]] and [[Massir]].
