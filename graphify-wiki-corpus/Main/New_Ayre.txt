'''New Ayre''' is a continent located in the northern and southern hemispheres. It is bordered by the [[Northern Eanif Ocean]] and the [[Sahare Gulf]] to the north, the [[Rianian Ocean]] to the east, [[Northern Eanif Ocean]] to the west, and the [[Southern Eanif Ocean]] to the south. The continent covers around {{convert|9,486,025|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of land, making it the 7th largest continent in the world, with a total of about 276,059,000 as of 1765, making it also the 7th largest continent by population behind [Massir]], [[Mahea]], [[Quia]], [[Acrola]], [[Ayuma]] and [[Atirha]].
