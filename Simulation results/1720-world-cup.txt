STORY OF THE TOURNAMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The 13th FLLA World Cup, hosted in Etirha across the breadth of Atirha,
became a celebration of open, expansive football. From the opening fixtures
it was clear that this would be a goal-rich tournament — visiting attacking
sides found space against deeper-sitting hosts and the average match topped
the historical norm by a comfortable margin.

Standing above the rest was Praesyu. Qualified through the AYFVL and seeded
into Group A, they rolled through the group stage with a measured, samba-
like attacking line: short passing, vertical bursts, full-backs pushed high.
They reached the knockout rounds unbeaten and proceeded to dispatch each
opponent with the same controlled aggression. Their semi-final was a
clinical display — a 4-1 dismantling that left observers calling them
the most complete attacking unit FLLA had ever seen.

The Final at the National Stadium in Etirha was, despite the billing, never
truly a contest. Praesyu opened the scoring early, doubled their lead before
half-time, and added two more in a punishing second half to seal a 4-0 win
— the largest winning margin in any FLLA World Cup final since 1660. Lycroa,
who had reached the final by way of the now-legendary semi-final, could find
no answer.

That semi-final was the match the tournament will be remembered for: a
thunderous 4-3 (a.e.t.) between Lycroa and Raledria, settled deep into
extra time with goals at both ends in the dying minutes. Already being called
the Game of the Century, it overshadowed everything else outside the champion's
march. Raledria fell agonisingly short but produced the individual
performance of the tournament — their attacking talisman took home the
Golden Boot with 11 goals despite their elimination.

Host Etirha (FLLA #10) topped Group C and reached the Quarter-Finals
before bowing out to Emara 2-4. A respectable showing
for the host nation in front of their own supporters.

Honourable mention to Sanagara (FLLA #13), the surprise package
from the qualifier pool, who toppled higher-ranked sides on their way to a
quarter-final exit.
