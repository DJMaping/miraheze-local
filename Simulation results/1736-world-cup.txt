CHAMPION: Raledria  (FLLA #1)  -  Fourth FLLA World Cup title (record)
   Final: Verusa 1-3 Raledria

THE FLOODLIGHT INCIDENT:
   The Verusa principal stadium floodlights failed in the 53rd minute of the
   final, suspending play for 36 minutes while engineers restored
   power. When play resumed, the visiting champions Raledria pushed on
   to seal the win against the home crowd in Verusa. The incident remains
   the longest in-match stoppage in FLLA World Cup final history.

   Verusa thus became the first nation to reach an FLLA World Cup final
   on home soil without lifting the trophy.

Podium:
   1st  Raledria  (FLLA #1)
   2nd  Verusa  (FLLA #4)  -  host nation
   3rd  Easuhura  (FLLA #5)
   4th  Etirha  (FLLA #10)

Golden Boot: Raledria  (15 goals, FLLA #1)

Tournament character:
   2.69 goals/match across 52 fixtures — among the most defensive
   editions in FLLA World Cup history. A tournament won by tactical discipline,
   organised pressing and set pieces. 2 knockout match(es) decided in extra time;
   3 additional knockout match(es) decided on penalties.
