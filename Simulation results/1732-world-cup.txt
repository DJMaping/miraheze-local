======================================================================
   1732 FLLA WORLD CUP - 16th EDITION
   Host: Dahe (Massir)
   First FLLA World Cup held in Massir
   Second edition contested at 24 teams (format reintroduced 1730)
   24-team format: 6 first-round groups + R16/QF knockout (1982 style)
======================================================================

CHAMPION: Lycroa  (FLLA #3)  -  Fourth FLLA World Cup title
   Final: Lycroa 2-2 (4-3 pen.) Emara
   At the Dahen national stadium.
   The match ended level at 2-2 after extra time and was settled on penalties —
   only the SECOND penalty shoot-out in FLLA World Cup history.

Podium:
   1st  Lycroa  (FLLA #3)
   2nd  Emara  (FLLA #2)
   3rd  Verusa  (FLLA #4)
   4th  Dahe  (FLLA #14)

Golden Boot: Verusa  (17 goals, FLLA #4)

Tournament character:
   3.90 goals/match across 52 fixtures — open, attacking football
   in the classical style of the era. Notable for clinical strikers and bold
   midfield play, in contrast to later, more defensive tournaments. A tense and
   tactical Final, however, produced the second penalty shoot-out in the
   competition's history.

Host nation run: Dahe topped their first-round group and went on to
   the Round of 16, advancing to the Quarter-Finals (Round of 8) — the
   landmark run for the inaugural Massir host nation. Final stage reached:
   Semi-Finals (4th place).
