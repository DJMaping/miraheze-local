----------------------------------------------------------------------
STORY OF THE TOURNAMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------

A celebration of the return to the 16-team format, this edition was
remembered for its open, attacking play from the visiting sides and the
iron-clad defensive composure of the host nation.

Emara strode through their six fixtures conceding only 0, and never
trailed in any match — a feat unmatched by any host nation before or since.
They scored 10 of their own, a measured tally from a side built on
patience, compact lines, and clinical counter-attacking. The final at the
principal venue in Emara's federal capital was settled by a single goal,
Taval unable to break through.

The Cinderella story of the tournament was Terressin (FLLA #20), a
debutant from a smaller confederation who toppled a Pot-1 opponent in the
group stage and pushed the eventual semi-finalists hard in the Quarter-Finals
before bowing out. Their performances drew comparison to Tunisia at the
1978 Earth tournament — a watershed moment for the federation back home.

The Emaran striker who claimed the Golden Boot was the standout individual,
pacing the leading scorers' chart from the opening fixtures to the final whistle.
