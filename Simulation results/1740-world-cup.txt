STORY OF THE TOURNAMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------

A defensive, tactical edition — a low-scoring grind with cagey 90-minute
battles, frequent extra-time stalemates, and penalty shoot-outs deciding
a remarkable 5 knockout ties.

The Cinderella story was Sattle (FLLA #117), an Acrolan
(Cfla) side widely written off before the tournament. They stunned a Pot-1
opponent in the Round of 16 to reach the Quarter-Finals — the deepest run by
a Cfla nation in FLLA World Cup history at that point.

Dahe (FLLA #15) produced their best-ever FLLA World Cup
performance, riding a solid back line to the Semi-Finals before losing to
eventual champions New Misos.

Host Seytinemas, riding the Mahean federations' new investment in the game,
put on a strong showing in front of a passionate home crowd at the Vulna
Olympic Stadium, where the final itself drew an estimated half a million
transcontinental radio listeners — the first FLLA WC match ever broadcast.
