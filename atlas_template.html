Calibration

Measured against Earth in 2015

1765 AFA maps to 2015 on Earth, so Earth's distribution is the yardstick. Every metric is a ratio or a share — the test is whether Andah's shape is one a real world produces, not its size.

One honest limitation. The gravity model that assigns trade between pairs cannot be validated with anything in Andah. It agrees closely with the world's flight network, but that network's demand is itself a gravity model over the same figures, so the agreement shows the two are consistent, not that gravity is the right structure. The metrics above are the genuinely external check.
